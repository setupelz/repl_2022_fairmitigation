Fair-share contributions to near-term mitigation investment

Regional cost-effective investment needs for 2020–2030 come from IPCC AR6 WGIII (Technical Summary, Figure TS.25). The tool redistributes the global total across the ten WGIII regions using your weighting of responsibility, capability and need, and shows the within-region and inter-regional contributions that result. Method and data: Pachauri et al. (2022), Science.
